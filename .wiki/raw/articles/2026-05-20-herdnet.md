# HerdNet

- License: MIT (per agent reporting)
- Stack: Python + PyTorch
- Stars: ~57
- Last update: Dec 2025
- Performance: 73-83% F1 on aerial livestock counting (paper-cited)

## What it is

A research-grade neural network for counting livestock and wildlife in aerial imagery. The closest published OSS analog to herd-scout's core herd-counting use case.

## Strengths

- Working livestock-specific aerial CV model (rare in OSS)
- MIT licensed
- Recently updated

## Weaknesses

- Counts only — doesn't manage records, doesn't integrate with FMS
- Research artifact, not production-grade
- Small community (57 stars)

## Relevance to herd-scout

HerdNet is what counts the cattle. **The product gap is bridging detection→herd inventory→record sync.** No OSS does this. herd-scout's wedge is exactly that integration: aerial CV count + reconcile against EID inventory + flag missing/sick animals.
