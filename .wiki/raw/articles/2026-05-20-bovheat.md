# BovHEAT

- License: MIT
- Stack: Python (Win/Mac/Linux executables)
- Stars: ~20
- Status: niche but production-quality

## What it is

Heat / estrus detection from SCR Heatime neck-collar accelerometer XLSX exports. Niche but it actually works because SCR exports XLSX (most competitors are closed-format).

## Why it matters

It's a rare example of OSS livestock sensor analytics that ships executables and works in production for its narrow use case.

## Limitation

Single sensor brand (SCR Heatime). Doesn't read tags directly — relies on SCR's app exporting XLSX.

## Relevance to herd-scout

Reference for "OSS livestock analytics that actually got built" but illustrative of the broader pain point: every commercial sensor (Allflex, Tru-Test, Datamars, Gallagher) is closed; OSS analytics only exists where vendors export usable formats.
