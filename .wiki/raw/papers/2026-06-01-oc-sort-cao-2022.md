# OC-SORT — observation-centric trajectory reconstruction

Pure motion-model tracker, **no ReID required**. Targets the case where SORT/ByteTrack fail: crowded scenes and non-linear motion.

## Key findings

- Core idea: **Observation-centric Re-Update (ORU)**. When a track is recovered after occlusion, OC-SORT goes back and re-updates the Kalman filter using the observations during the gap, preventing the error accumulation that breaks SORT/ByteTrack on non-linear paths.
- Adds **observation-centric momentum** (OCM) and **observation-centric recovery** (OCR) on top of SORT — all motion-only.
- Benchmarks: **DanceTrack 89.4 MOTA** (DanceTrack is the canonical non-linear-motion benchmark), MOT17/20 HOTA 63.2 / 62.4.
- Inference: **~28 FPS end-to-end** on RTX 2080 Ti; **~700 FPS for the association step alone on CPU**. Drop-in compute cost vs ByteTrack is negligible.
- **Deep OC-SORT** (arXiv:2302.11813) adds Adaptive CMC + Dynamic Appearance and tops MOT20 (63.9 HOTA). CMC is the moving-camera feature; **Dynamic Appearance** is the relevant addition for static chute/pasture video.

## Implications for herd-scout

This is the strongest theoretical fit for **cattle bunching at gates / feed bunks** — the case where ByteTrack's linear-Kalman assumption breaks. Negligible compute overhead means it's a cheap A/B candidate alongside the existing ByteTrack baseline. Test via [[boxmot-sam2-tooling|BoxMOT]] without rewriting the sidecar pipeline.
