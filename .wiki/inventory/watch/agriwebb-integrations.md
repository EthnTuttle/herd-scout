# Watch: AgriWebb integrations / partner directory

## Why this is on the watch list

AgriWebb has 17k+ producers across AU/NZ/UK/US/ZA — the largest livestock-specific OSS-or-not footprint. They already integrate with EID hardware (Allflex, Datamars, Tru-Test). The fastest way for them to commoditise herd-scout's drone-vision wedge is a thin partnership with Roboflow / DroneDeploy / a dedicated CV vendor that adds "fly drone → confirmed mob count" as a feature. Watch the integrations / partner directory quarterly.

## Watch triggers

Re-evaluate when any of:

- A new CV / drone-counting integration appears in AgriWebb's partner directory
- A Roboflow Universe or Microsoft AI for Good livestock workspace gets named in AgriWebb marketing
- AgriWebb acquires or invests in a CV/drone vendor
- AgriWebb launches an in-house drone or CV SKU
- AgriWebb opens an OSS or self-hostable tier (would change the strategic calculus)

## Cadence

Quarterly check on https://www.agriwebb.com/ integrations / news / blog. If a CV/drone partnership lands, fold it into the assess re-run that quarter.
